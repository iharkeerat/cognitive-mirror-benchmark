# Ethics and safeguards

## Principal risk

Language models can offer highly confident psychological stories about individuals from weak evidence. A benchmark that forces any one psychological interpretation may repeat this error rather than measure it.

## Proposed safeguards

- **No clinical use:** no diagnostic label, therapy recommendation, psychiatric triage or mental-health risk scoring.
- **No consequential profiling:** no lie detection, employee surveillance, school discipline, criminal-intent judgement or eligibility decisions.
- **Privacy first:** do not use identifiable private testimony, confidential records or sensitive personal data in public examples.
- **Evidence-relative labels:** evaluate what the text warrants, not a person's unobservable private thoughts.
- **Valid abstention:** declining unsupported speculation can be the appropriate response.
- **Language and culture:** assess assumptions about dialect, translation and social norms; seek domain expertise.
- **Contestability:** preserve disagreement and permit exclusion of irreducibly ambiguous items.
- **Oversight:** seek ethics and specialist review before participant-facing research.

## No deployment claim

This repository is a research-protocol scaffold, **not a tool for evaluating real people**. It must not be used to make consequential decisions about individuals.
