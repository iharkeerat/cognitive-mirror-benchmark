# Limitations and non-claims

1. **No assembled benchmark.** The 1,500–3,000 item and ≥30% negative figures are planned targets.
2. **No independent human annotations.** The plan for three annotators per item has not been executed.
3. **No frozen dataset split or held-out evaluation.** No model accuracy, calibration or benchmark improvement is reported.
4. **No validated psychological inference.** Textual support is different from the true inner state of any person.
5. **No completed ethics, representativeness or clinical review.** Future source collection requires appropriate oversight.
6. **Provisional schema.** The four support statuses and sample format are engineering choices, not independently validated categories.
7. **No production tool.** Code included here validates toy-example structure; it does not predict psychological traits or states.
8. **No institutional endorsement.** Author's educational affiliation is separate from independent project sponsorship.
9. **AI assistance.** Public documentation and scaffold were prepared with AI assistance under author direction; author review and correction are necessary.
